# Gear Transfer & the ilvl 70 Keeper Rule

Gear Transfer is how AION 2 lets you move upgrade investment from one piece of gear into another — and it is also the single most common way new players burn Kinah for nothing. This guide covers what actually transfers, what silently disappears, and why the community treats **item level 70** as the first tier worth investing in.

## What Gear Transfer moves (and what it eats)

Transfer is one-directional and destructive: the **donor item is consumed**, and the destination receives the investment. Community-verified rules:

**Carried over:**

- Enhancement level (re-applied against the new base)
- **Soul Binding** lines — the item's substat set
- Amplification (per Game8, relayed through community testing)

**Lost forever:**

- **Potential** — the item's random stat roll
- **Manastones** — unsocket them first
- **Theostones** — the weapon proc
- The **Philosopher's Stone** bonus line (community tests report it drops)

Three more constraints matter as much as the lists above:

- Transfer **unlocks at character level 45**.
- Both items must be **soul-bound to the same character**.
- The **destination cannot already have a fixed Soul Binding** — you cannot overwrite a locked substat set.

Line counts follow fixed rules too: transferring a 4-line item into a 5-line item **rolls the 5th line free**, while a 5-line item transferred into a 3-line item **keeps only the first three** — the rest are gone.

## Why ilvl 70 is the "first keeper tier"

Here is the mechanic that explains the whole community phrase: **soul-binding lines transfer as a percentage of the item's maximum roll, not as a flat value.** The same line keeps the same percentage — but an ilvl 70 item has a higher ceiling, so the same "80% roll" is worth more absolute stats on an ilvl 70 base than on an ilvl 54 base.

That produces the classic trap: a player pushes a great roll from a low-tier item into their first ilvl 70 piece, sees *"the stats didn't go up,"* and decides Transfer is broken. It isn't — the percentage held, the ceiling just rose.

So the community's practical workflow is the opposite of what new players assume:

1. **Roll substats on cheap gold (Epic) gear** — Epic transfers are a stopgap, not a destination.
2. **Transfer the roll into your first ilvl 70 keeper** — the gear you'll actually keep enhancing.
3. **Never deep-invest below ilvl 70.** The first keeper tier starts at item level 70: **Aulamus weapons and accessories** from Urugugu Canyon, **Vakron armor** from Vakron Sky Island, and the crafted **T1.5 "Splendant Star Dragon Lord"** set. Crafted tiers run T1 (ilvl 62) up to T3.5 (ilvl 102), so ilvl 70 is the *first* long-term stop — not the last.

## The safe upgrade workflow

1. **Post-story (~700–750 Gear Score):** clear the story, wear what drops, invest nothing.
2. **Roll substats on cheap gold gear** while you farm 1★–2★ expeditions (Krao Cave / Draupnir for the ilvl 54 band).
3. **Hit 1,400 GS and farm Urugugu (weapons) + Vakron (armor).** These are your ilvl 70 keepers — transfer your rolled substats in.
4. **Enhance the keeper to +10 safely** — Unique enhancement steps succeed up to +10; only the last five steps (Unique +10 → +15) can fail.
5. **Push +10 → +15 with pity math:** reported success rates are 65 / 50 / 35 / 25 / 20%, each failure at the same level adds roughly **+5% soft pity** (reset on success), and the item is **never destroyed on failure** — you lose Kinah and Enhance Stones only. The sole downgrade exception is the **Clash Rune**, which drops a level on failure.
6. **Endgame:** transfer forward into ilvl 86 (Fire Temple / Ferocious Horn Den gear) and eventually the ilvl 102 ceiling — Ludra's raid set and the crafted Splendent pieces, with Amplified Enhancement (+5 levels past +15) and Potential (4 PvP-only levels) on top.

## Common Kinah traps

<BossMechanicAlert title="Read this before you press Confirm">

- **Enhancing below ilvl 70** — that investment is stranded; transfer it forward and lose the Potential roll anyway.
- **Transferring into a piece with a fixed Soul Binding** — the game refuses; plan which piece carries the binding.
- **The 5→3 line loss** — moving a 5-line roll into a 3-line item deletes two lines permanently.
- **Removing Theostones** — community reports say they are destroyed on removal; socket decisions are semi-permanent.
- **Extracting for stones** — extraction refunds Enhance Stones but **not Kinah**, and gear **cannot be sold to NPCs**, so dead items are only useful as Transfer donors.
- **Trusting any of this blindly** — NCSoft has published **no official rules page** for Transfer. Every number here is community-derived from the live client; read the preview window every single time.

</BossMechanicAlert>

## Costs to expect (community figures)

- An **ilvl 86 Nuakum Helm from +0 to +10**: roughly **30,920 Enhancement Stones + 775,100 Kinah**.
- An **ilvl 102 piece from +0 to +15**: about **15.0M Kinah + ~190,700 stones on average**; the worst-case +10→+15 stretch alone is estimated at **~73.9M Kinah**.

Budget accordingly — and remember that every Kinah you spend below ilvl 70 is Kinah you'll spend again at the keeper tier.

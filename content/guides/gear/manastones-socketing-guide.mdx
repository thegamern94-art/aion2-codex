# Manastones, Soulstones & Theostones

Socketing is AION 2's main-stat min-max layer — and the highest error-cost system in the game: merges fail, stones are destroyed, and the weekly caps force real decisions. Here is the whole pipeline in one page.

## The three stone families

- **Manastones** — socket into weapons and armor. 9 stat effects across grades from Basic to Ultimate.
- **Soulstones** — the accessory equivalent, with their own line pool.
- **Theostones** — the merge/upgrade material, plus the one **proc slot** on Epic and Unique weapons and guards (51 exist, all weapon-category, e.g. Tiamat's Rage: 1% chance, 15,000 fire damage).

Socket capacity scales with item grade — **Rare 2, Unique 4** (the Heroic/Mythic grades don't exist on Global yet) — plus one Theostone proc slot on Epic/Unique weapons and guards.

## The merge odds are brutal

- Rare → Epic: **11.12%**
- Epic → Unique: **9.10%**

<BossMechanicAlert title="Read before you socket">

- **Superior manastones are capped at 5 per week per character** in the dungeon shop — plan which stats you're buying before the reset.
- Merges consume stones on failure at those odds; budget for the expected value, not the lucky case.
- Theostones are **destroyed on removal** — a socket decision is semi-permanent.
- **Manastones and Soulstones do NOT survive Gear Transfer** — a transferred piece arrives with empty sockets.

</BossMechanicAlert>

## What actually rolls

- Ordinary manastones roll the basic pool: Attack, Crit Hit, Accuracy, HP, MP, Defense, Evasion, Crit Resist, Block.
- Only **Superior** stones roll the premium multiplier lines: Damage Boost, Weapon Damage Boost, Critical Damage Boost, and positional **Front/Back Attack Damage Boost**.
- One yellow damage line = **100 Boost = 1%** of that stat on your sheet — premium lines are worth ~2.5% even at low quality, which is why Superior stones are the endgame chase.
- **Double Chance** (community: "Smite") is excellent on several classes — and **never rolls on manastones**. It comes from passives, Pantheon decorations, pets and other systems.
- Sheet **Crit caps at 50%** on Global, and class buffs count toward it: cover the cap, then stop buying Crit.
- Accuracy, Crit and Combat Speed are "until it works" stats — cover the breakpoint the content requires, then move budget to multipliers.
- PvP uses a separate pool entirely: **Abyssal Manastones** (PvP Damage Boost, PvP Accuracy, Status Effect Resist, PvP Tolerance) from the AP shop.

## Per-class priorities

The full per-class breakdown lives in each class's <Link href="/classes/">Class Codex tab</Link> — the short version: positional amps lead for front-position melee (Templar/Gladiator) and back-position burst (Assassin), casters weigh Weapon Damage vs the Front-Attack caveat (ground effects skip it), Spiritmaster stays deliberately flexible, and Cleric is a genuine role-split between damage-healer and full-support framings.

## Alchemy feeds all of this

**Alchemy crafts Manastones and Soulstones** — the socketing economy and the crafting system are the same economy. If you're deciding a second profession or an alt's specialty, the stone supply chain is the highest-leverage crafting lane for market income.

## The weekly decision

Because Superior stones are capped at 5/week/character, socketing is a *weekly planning* problem, not a shopping spree: pick the two lines your build actually scales with, buy those five, merge deliberately, and track the cap on the <Link href="/checklist/">Weekly Checklist</Link>.

# Skill Specializations: Equip Them Yourself

AION 2 has a second skill layer most new players miss entirely: **Skill Specializations** — extra modifiers you slot on top of your normal skills. They unlock early, they're free to change, and the game will not equip them for you.

## Where they live

Open **Skills (K) → Mastery → Specialty**. Slots unlock at character levels **8 / 12 / 16 / 20** — four specialty slots by level 20, which is also where the choices start mattering.

## The mistake everyone makes

Specializations **must be equipped by hand**. Unlocking one does not put it on your skill — you have to drag or select it into a specialty slot. Players routinely level to 45 with empty specialty slots and wonder why their damage feels flat.

<BossMechanicAlert title="Check this right now">

Open K → Mastery → Specialty and confirm all four slots are filled. If a slot is empty, you are leaving a free, permanent upgrade on the table — no currency, no grind, just a click.

</BossMechanicAlert>

## Presets 1–3

Specializations support **three presets**. Treat them as loadouts:

1. **PvE preset** — single-target and AoE specialties for dungeon content.
2. **PvP preset** — control, mobility and burst modifiers for the Abyss and Battlefields.
3. **Farm preset** — whatever maximizes clear speed for dailies and Sealed Dungeons.

Switching presets changes which specializations are active — so after a swap, re-check that your intended specializations are still equipped in the new preset.

## Per-class picks

Which specialties are actually worth taking differs by class. Our **Meta Build tab** on every [class page](/classes/) shows the specialties top Korean players run, with icons and pick rates — start there, then verify on the training dummies.

## Resets and mistakes

- You can **reset specialty choices** — it is not a permanent character decision (unlike class choice).
- The classic post-reset mistake: resetting, then forgetting to re-equip. The slots go back to empty, not to a default.
- Specializations are **per ability-preset**, like Stigma learning — changing presets is not the same as changing specialties.

## The 30-second checklist

1. K → Mastery → Specialty.
2. Four slots by level 20 — fill all four.
3. Re-check after every preset swap.
4. Compare your picks against the Meta Build tab for your class.

That's the whole system — cheap to do, expensive to forget. Data on unlock levels and presets documented by [daevaguides.com](https://daevaguides.com) (October 2026); verify current values in-game after patches.

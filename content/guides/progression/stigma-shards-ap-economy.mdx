# Stigma & the AP Economy

Stigma is AION 2's skill-enhancement system — and the single biggest post-45 power gate. Every point of Abyss Points (AP) you farm eventually converts into Stigma levels, so this guide covers both halves: how Stigma costs work, and where AP actually comes from.

## How Stigma works

- Stigma unlocks at **level 22**; each class has **13 stigmas** with a maximum of **4 equipped** in slots that open at character levels **22 / 27 / 32 / 37**.
- Leveling a stigma costs Stigma Shards in bands: **1 / 2 / 4 / 8 shards** per level band — roughly **75 shards to reach Lv 20** on one stigma.
- Beyond Lv 20 you need **Superior Stigma Shards** (endgame currency tier).
- Skills learned are tied to **ability presets** — and that multiplies your costs.

<BossMechanicAlert title="The per-preset trap">

Learning a stigma is **per ability-preset**. If you run a PvE preset and a PvP preset, the same stigma costs twice. Decide your preset strategy before buying shards, not after.

</BossMechanicAlert>

## Where Stigma Shards come from

1. **Level-up rewards:** 30 shards by level 45 — enough to start, nowhere near enough to finish.
2. **Abyss Trade Shop → Growth tab** (unlocks at Lv 45): 4 tiers × 50 shards, priced **10,000 / 15,000 / 20,000 / 25,000 AP** — roughly **200 shards per character for ~70,000 AP total**. This is the main faucet.
3. **Festival Shop:** 300 Centuryroot → 35 shards (per character).
4. **Nightmare Shop:** 1,500 Phantasmal Fragments → 35 shards (per character).

## The AP engine

Where the Abyss Points for all of the above come from:

| Source | AP | Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duty missions | ~1,000 each | 5 rewards / day |
| Supply Requests | ~380–6,000 | Variable |
| Arena of Tactics | ~1,000 (~3,000 for a win) | ~3× / week |
| Arena of Solitude / Cooperation | varies | ~10× / week |
| **Abyss Sieges** | **~45,000 bursts** | **Mon / Thu / Sat, 12:00 UTC** |

Community measurement at level 45 lands at **~104,000–127,500 AP per week** once PvE limits are exhausted (Corridor farming, converting into Commands, then into Stigma Shards). A weekly AP cap exists — NCSoft just hasn't published the number.

## Abyss entry is now a budget

The **Oct 14, 2026 patch** changed Abyss entry into a capped accumulated currency: **max 7 hours accumulated entry per week** (Season Missions moved to the Abyss tab and now pay Abyss Entry Stones). One source describes three Reshanta layers with 7 hours *each* per week — that conflicts with the patch note's total — so treat the exact accounting as **verify in-game**.

Sieges are still the week's biggest paydays: the **artifact fight runs Mon / Thu / Sat at 12:00 UTC** — arrive early, because positioning at the artifact decides the burst.

## A weekly AP plan that actually finishes

1. **Daily:** clear your 5 Duty missions (~5,000 AP/day plus the Bound Kinah).
2. **Siege days (Mon/Thu/Sat):** show up early — one siege ≈ half a week's income.
3. **Arena windows:** Solitude/Cooperation ~10× per week are quick AP on top.
4. **Spend deliberately:** Growth-tab shards first (the power gate), then Abyssal manastones for PvP stats.
5. **Track it:** the Weekly Checklist has the whole loop — including the 7-hour Abyss budget.

The bottom line: at 45, your power ceiling is a currency conversion problem — and now you know the exchange rates.

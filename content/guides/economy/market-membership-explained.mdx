# Market, Membership & the Daeva Pass, Explained

AION 2's economy has two gates that every player hits in the first month: the **Market requires membership**, and the **Daeva Pass bills per character**. This page is the honest decision framework — what each purchase actually unlocks, and what the October patches changed.

## What the Special Quai Membership unlocks

**$14.99 / 30 days**, and it is **server-bound**:

- **Market and Quna Exchange access** — without it, you cannot list or buy. There is no player trading without membership.
- **Remote Storage** — bank access from anywhere.
- **Wind Breeze** — movement QoL.
- **Higher Odyle cap: 840 instead of 560** — that's +50% dungeon reward throughput for the heaviest players.

The stated design line is that cosmetics confer no combat advantage — the paid advantages are economy and QoL. Whether that framing survives contact with the Odyle cap is a judgment call; the cap difference is real throughput.

## Trading rules that surprise people

- **Player-to-player direct trades are disabled** (anti-bot measure). All value moves through the Marketplace — which means through membership.
- The **Oct 14, 2026 patch** added a listing restriction on **gathering materials based on gameplay history** — an anti-RMT/bot measure that directly hits gatherer-seller workflows. Check your listing eligibility before stockpiling materials to sell.

<BossMechanicAlert title="Check before you hoard">

If your income plan is "gather materials → sell on Market", verify that your account can actually list gathering materials after the Oct 14 restriction. A stockpile you can't list is dead inventory — and there are no player-to-player trades to fall back on.

</BossMechanicAlert>

- KR precedent (Dec 2025): a **World Exchange with a 20% tax** and membership-only exchanges — Global adoption status is unverified, but it's the direction of travel to watch.

## The Daeva Pass math (for roster players)

- **Season 1 Premium Daeva Pass: 1,500 Quna per character** — four alts means four passes.
- **Season 1 ends December 16, 2026** — pass rewards and ranking titles have a hard deadline.
- Ranking titles carry **no stats** (prestige only) — the stat titles come from collections, not rankings.

## Regular vs Bound Kinah (the tie-in)

The Market runs on **Regular (tradeable) Kinah**. Duties and quests pay **Bound Kinah** — spendable, never tradeable. If your upgrade plan depends on Market purchases, your income plan must include Conquest expeditions, raid kills, or marketable crafts — not just dailies. The full breakdown with verified numbers lives in the <Link href="/kinah/">Kinah Hub</Link>.

## So… should you subscribe?

A reasonable framework:

1. **You want to sell anything** — crafts, farmed materials, gear drops → membership is effectively required; the rest is a bonus.
2. **You run 4+ characters weekly** — the Odyle cap (560 → 840) is real throughput; membership pays for itself in cube value if you actually cap out.
3. **You only play one character casually** — skip it; Bound Kinah from Duties covers progression sinks, and you lose only Market access.
4. **Daeva Pass** — buy it on the character you play most, and only if you'll reach the reward tiers before **Dec 16**. Per-character pricing means alts rarely justify it.

Prices and terms are as documented in October 2026 — verify the current store page before purchasing.

# Space-Time Rifts: Windows, Loot & Survival

The **Space-Time Rift** is AION 2's recurring cross-faction incursion: portals open on a fixed cycle and send Elyos into Asmodian home regions — and vice versa. It is the **only way to enter the opposing faction's territory**, it is an open-world PvPvE zone **separate from the Abyss**, and it is where the best reward-per-minute quests in the overworld live.

## Windows and timers

- Rifts open **every 3 hours, around the clock**.
- Commonly cited windows: **02:00 · 05:00 · 08:00 · 11:00 · 14:00 · 17:00 · 20:00 · 23:00 UTC** (the UTC anchor is community-derived — confirm against your in-game timer; the live header on this site counts down to the next window).
- A portal stays **usable for 10 minutes** once it opens; once inside you can **remain up to 1 hour**.
- Each portal caps at **240 players**; when it fills, a new portal spawns elsewhere in the region.
- The **Oct 8, 2026 patch** raised simultaneous portals to **2 per region / 6 per race**.

## What you actually do inside

- Run the **7 regional quests per faction** (green markers) — the best reward-per-minute in the activity.
- Farm **enemy-faction gathering nodes and monsters** at your own risk.
- Run **enemy Sealed Dungeons**, capture **strongholds**, and kill **field bosses** that are normally unreachable.
- Collect the payoff: **Kinah, Abyss Points and enhancement stones**.

## The two things that kill you

<BossMechanicAlert title="Read before your first rift">

- **Enemy NPC guards** at outposts and near Kibelisks attack on sight and can **one-shot** under-geared players. Route around them, never through.
- **Death sends you back to your home continent.** The return trip — re-entering through a new portal window — is the real cost. Bank your quest turn-ins before you take risks.

</BossMechanicAlert>

PvP itself is **opt-in**: you cannot be attacked until you attack a player or a flagged target. Unflagged farmers are safe from players — but not from guards, and not from their own greed.

## Survival playbook

1. **Plant your own Kibelisk** in a quiet corner as a respawn anchor before you start farming.
2. **Group up** — a duo with a Templar running the <SkillTooltip id="templar-aggro-bulwark" /> loop can hold a guard chokepoint while you finish a quest.
3. **Stay unflagged** until you consciously decide to fight. Flagging is a choice; guards don't care either way.
4. **Route around outposts and Kibelisk zones.** The one-shot guard radius is bigger than it looks.
5. **Get home early** via the map teleport or a return scroll instead of riding out the full 1-hour timer — every extra minute inside is another minute someone can find you.
6. **Bank quests often.** Dying with completed, unturned quests is the most expensive way to learn rule #2.

## Rewards, and the Abyss confusion

Rift rewards — **Kinah, Abyss Points, enhancement stones** — plus the regional quests make rifts a core weekly loop. The Oct 14, 2026 patch added **Lower Abyss Rift Stones** to the Halloween event shop and a parallel **"Conquerors of the Abyss"** event (Oct 14–28), which is why guides keep conflating the two systems:

- **Space-Time Rift** = cross-faction overworld incursion, every 3 hours, PvP opt-in, guards one-shot.
- **The Abyss** = a separate endgame zone entered via Abyss Rift Stones / entry stones.

They're separate places with separate rules — but both feed the same PvP currency, which is why most players stack them into a single farming session.

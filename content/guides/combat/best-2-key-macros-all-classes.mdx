# Best 2-Key Macro Slots for All 8 Classes

AION 2 ships an **official in-game Skill Macro editor** — no third-party tools, no ambiguity about legality. This guide shows you how the system actually works and gives you copy-paste slot setups for every launch class.

## How the official macro system works

- Open the **Skill menu (K) → Skill Macro** (upper right).
- Register **up to 4 skills per macro slot**. **The bottom slot is the highest priority** — if a skill's cooldown or condition is unmet, the game auto-falls upward to the next one.
- **Hold the macro key** to cycle-execute the registered sequence.
- The macro key is **unbound by default**: enable it via **Settings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay/Skill Macro**.
- Official tuning guidance: **~10–20 ms delay on low ping, ~40–50 ms above ~80–100 ms ping**, and **turn the separate Skill Queue option OFF** while using macros so the two systems don't fight.
- Put the **longest-cooldown skill first** in the slot, and keep **long-CD buffs, Stigmas, movement and defensive skills manual**.
- Third-party automation is **not sanctioned** — community reports describe bans for unauthorized tools (including DPS meters). Stick to the built-in editor.

The copy boxes below use that slot format. Each class gets two setups: a **damage loop** you can safely automate, and a **manual key** for the things that must never be macroed.

## What never goes in a macro

<BossMechanicAlert title="Official guidance — keep these manual">

- **Long-cooldown buffs, Stigmas, movement and defensive skills** — the game's own setup guidance calls these out; automating them wastes their exact timing.
- **The separate Skill Queue** — turn it OFF while macros are active, or the two systems fight over your presses.
- **Anything from outside the game** — third-party automation is not sanctioned, and community reports describe bans for unauthorized tools. The built-in editor is the whole legal toolbox.
- **Healing decisions** (Cleric's big reactive heals) — a macro that fires your emergency heal on a timer is how raids lose their Cleric's cooldowns at the wrong second.

</BossMechanicAlert>

## Tuning checklist

1. Bind the macro key: **Settings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay/Skill Macro**.
2. Build the slot **bottom-up**: longest cooldown first, fillers after.
3. Set **delay 10–20 ms** on low ping; **40–50 ms** above ~80–100 ms.
4. Turn **Skill Queue OFF**.
5. Practice the hold-to-cycle rhythm on trash before taking it into a Sanctuary raid.
6. Re-tune after balance patches — the Oct 2026 pass (Cleric heal nerf, Chanter PvP nerf) is exactly the kind of change that shifts optimal slot order.
